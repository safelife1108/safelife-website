About Safe Life Diagnostic Centre

Safe Life Diagnostic Centre has been serving patients since 1999 with a strong commitment to accuracy, reliability, and affordable healthcare. With over 26 years of experience, modern diagnostic equipment, and trained professionals, we ensure every patient receives precise reports and compassionate care.

Our mission is simple: to deliver trustworthy diagnostic services that help doctors and patients make the right medical decisions.
